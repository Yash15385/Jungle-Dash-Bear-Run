## Brand & Style
The brand personality captures the exhilarating speed and optimism of an endless desert runner bathed in radiant midday sun. It blends the kinetic punch of classic arcade gaming with the breezy, crisp polish of modern sports-utility software. The target audience comprises arcade players, casual gamers, and leaderboard competitors who seek instant visual clarity, punchy responsiveness, and an infectious sense of momentum.

The design movement mixes **Tactile Arcade Realism** with **Frosted Modern Minimalist Surfaces**. Surfaces are aerodynamic, shaped with full pill curvatures and buoyant, buoyant layered elevations. The visual tone is brisk, optimistic, and luminous: sun-drenched sky gradients, high-contrast navy glyphs, and high-visibility citrus energy triggers that beg to be tapped.

## Colors
The palette evokes an open highway under cloudless azure skies, anchored by deep contrast and intense velocity markers:

- **Primary Action (Sunshine Amber / Velocity Orange):** `#ea580c` driving primary buttons, with `#f59e0b` and `#fbbf24` serving as radiant kinetic highlights, active states, and streak counters.
- **Secondary Atmospheric (Sky & Azure):** `#0284c7` anchors active game chrome, progress rings, and fast-forward controls.
- **Tertiary Accent (Cyan / Energetic Teal):** `#06b6d4` and `#0d9488` illuminate high-score pings, speed boosts, multiplier meters, and live leaderboard status indicators.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces (Deep Navy on Cloud White):**
  - Text Primary: `#0c1938` delivers absolute legibility during high-speed transitions.
  - Text Secondary: `#1e293b` for auxiliary stats, metrics, and secondary copy.
  - Background Base: `#e2eefb` (soft daylight haze).
  - Elevated Containers: `#ffffff` (crisp white) layered over `#f0f7ff` (feathered cloud blue).
  - Outlines & Borders: `#bfdbfe` at 60% opacity for crisp edge definition without heavy visual weight.

## Typography
The typography marries the technical, forward-thrusting geometry of `Space Grotesk` with the warm, ultra-readable humanism of `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

- **Headlines & Metric Readouts:** `Space Grotesk` carries uppercase energy, tight tracking, and punchy numeric forms ideal for distance covered, multiplier tallies, and rank titles.
- **Body & Dialogue:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides soft, approachable counters that ensure instructions, character perks, and system text remain effortless to absorb under motion blur or fast scanning.
- **Labels & Micro-data:** Set in uppercase `Space Grotesk` with widened letter-spacing for tactical arcade HUD telemetry.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout relies on a fluid grid designed for rapid portrait one-handed play on mobile and expansive HUD telemetry on wider devices:

- **Mobile Viewports (<640px):** 4-column layout with 16px margins and 12px gutters. Primary HUD elements dock to the bottom 30% thumb zone and the top safe-margin bar.
- **Tablet / Mid Displays (640px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with 24px margins and 16px gutters. Dashboard and stage selection cards arrange in 2-column modular grids.
- **Desktop / Wide Layouts (>1024px):** 12-column layout max-width bounded at 1280px with 40px outer margins. The central viewport preserves game focus while collapsible side wings house telemetry, live tournament brackets, and achievement feeds.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Built strictly around 4px / 8px increments. Compact spacing inside badges (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) balances against airy macro spacing between game zones (`space-lg` to `space-xl`).

## Elevation & Depth
Depth simulates sunlight cast onto glossy aerodynamic materials rather than dark drop-shadows:

- **Ambient Sun-Cast Shadows:** Shadows are warm-tinted and luminous using low-density sky blue tones (`rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.12)`) and saturated amber under glows (`rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.28)` for active CTA buttons) rather than muddy grey.
- **Frosted Translucency:** Floating panels, pause overlays, and leaderboard rails use background blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) mixed with 88% opaque `#ffffff` and `#f0f7ff`.
- **Gloss Rim Highlights:** Top-level containers feature an inner hairline highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`) paired with a crisp outer edge (`1px solid rgba(191, 219, 254, 0.6)`), giving surfaces the feel of coated acrylic speed-badges.
- **Tactile Pressed State:** Interactive elements extrude downward by 3px via solid bottom offsets (`box-shadow: 0 4px 0 #c2410c` for primary buttons), which compress flush to `0px` with a slight translate on press.

## Shapes
The shape language is aerodynamic, frictionless, and pill-dominant (`level 3` roundedness):

- **Full Capsule Elements:** Buttons, search pills, stage tags, and speed meters use continuous full pill radii (`border-radius: 9999px`) to reinforce momentum and eliminate sharp velocity catches.
- **Nested Card Containers:** Large interface cards adopt `rounded-xl` (48px / 3rem) exterior corners, with internal sub-panels stepping down to `rounded-lg` (32px / 2rem) for proportional nested curvature.
- **Geometric Accents:** Micro-accents (chevron indicators, nitro notches, stage progress diamonds) utilize 45-degree angle caps to echo arcade speed lines.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Arcade CTA ("Run", "Boost", "Claim"):** Pill-shaped, gradient fill from `#fbbf24` to `#ea580c`, bright white Space Grotesk bold text, extruded 3px bottom edge (`#c2410c`), and an ambient sunshine glow (`box-shadow: 0 8px 20px rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary Action ("Equip", "Stats"):** Frosted cloud white (`#ffffff`), 1px sky border (`#bfdbfe`), deep navy text (`#0c1938`), and instant cyan hover rim (`#06b6d4`).
- **Icon / Utility Actions:** Circular 48x48px pill buttons with high-contrast navy icons on translucent `#f0f7ff` base.

### Chips & Badges
- **Multiplier & Speed Tags:** Full capsule pills, `#0284c7` or `#0d9488` backgrounds with high-contrast white text, uppercase `label-sm` tracking, and an energetic lightning or flame prefix icon.
- **Status Indicators:** Pill-shaped, frosted background with a pulsing cyan or amber live status dot.

### Lists & Leaderboards
- **Leaderboard Rows:** Horizontal floating pill cards with `#ffffff` base, alternating subtle tint `#f0f7ff`, separated by `space-sm` gaps. Rank numbers 1-3 display metallic sunshine amber and cyan badges.
- **Player Strip:** Pinned bottom pill bar with translucent frost blur, featuring current user rank, avatar silhouette, and high-visibility neon distance ticker.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches
- **Toggles:** Pill-shaped runner track in `#bfdbfe` switching to dynamic cyan (`#06b6d4`) when active. Thumb is an elevated glossy white sphere with smooth spring motion.
- **Checkboxes:** Heavily rounded squircle (8px radius) with `#0284c7` fill and white checkmark icon when selected.

### Input Fields
- **Search & Name Fields:** Capsule-profile input shells in `#ffffff`, subtle inset shadow, bordered with `#bfdbfe`. Focused state highlights with a 2px energetic teal ring (`#06b6d4`) and faint ambient daylight glow.

### Cards & Modular Surfaces
- **Stage & Character Cards:** Built with crisp `#ffffff` surfaces over `#e2eefb` backdrops. Enclosed by subtle 1px border (`#bfdbfe` at 60%). Features an upper preview display with radiant sky gradients and lower telemetry readouts set in deep navy typography.

### Specialized Arcade HUD Elements
- **Velocity Gauge:** Semi-circular pill meter with segmented cyan-to-orange progress ticks.
- **Feather-Drop Coin Counter:** Pill-shaped frosted widget with embedded sun-amber coin insignia and tabular `Space Grotesk` numbering.
